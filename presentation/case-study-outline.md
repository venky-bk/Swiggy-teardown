# 15-slide portfolio case-study outline

1. **Swiggy's product challenge is confidence, not speed alone** — scope, thesis, research cut-off. Visual: one-line value loop. Evidence: official product sources.
2. **The food marketplace coordinates four interdependent actors** — consumer, restaurant, delivery partner, platform. Visual: ecosystem map.
3. **Different occasions create different jobs** — time-constrained, budget-led, dietary-goal, group organiser. Visual: segment cards; label as hypotheses.
4. **The core loop spans discovery through recovery** — journey stages and critical moments. Visual: journey map.
5. **UX hypotheses need validation before redesign** — price clarity, ETA uncertainty, menu-data confidence, recovery. Visual: issue-evidence-confidence matrix.
6. **Competition is local and operational as well as digital** — marketplace vs direct ordering/substitutes. Visual: comparison matrix.
7. **Marketplace economics can conflict with surface-level conversion** — contribution, incentives, support, partner impact. Visual: order-level contribution equation.
8. **A North Star must reflect fulfilled value** — proposed satisfactory fulfilled orders and guardrails. Visual: KPI tree.
9. **Measurement gaps are the biggest research constraint** — event taxonomy, funnel, cohorts. Visual: funnel.
10. **AI is relevant where it removes real friction** — public MCP/voice context; avoid assuming proprietary architecture. Visual: AI opportunity decision tree.
11. **Meal Confidence is the proposed MVP** — price, ETA confidence, menu provenance, recovery. Visual: feature concept.
12. **The design is source-of-truth first** — existing services, validation, analytics, safe fallbacks. Visual: proposed architecture.
13. **Five experiments can test value before scaling** — price clarity, ETA, dietary provenance, recovery, conversational discovery. Visual: experiment cards.
14. **Roadmap stages risk and investment** — research, prototype, MVP, pilot, evaluate. Visual: phased timeline.
15. **Scale only when user value and economics improve together** — recommendation, stop conditions, limitations. Visual: decision checklist.

For each slide, cite sources in a footer or speaker notes and label hypothetical metrics. Do not use screenshots or logos unless authorised or appropriately attributed.
