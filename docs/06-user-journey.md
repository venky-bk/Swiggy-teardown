# 06 — User journey map

This is a **hypothesised journey** based on the normal structure of a food-delivery marketplace; it is not a claim that the author performed a live app walkthrough.

| Stage | User goal | Likely friction to investigate | Metric | Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Remember a useful option | Low relevance of marketing | Qualified visit rate | Occasion-based messaging with consent |
| Acquisition | Open app and choose address | Location/address setup | Address completion | Clear address validation |
| Discovery | Find suitable food | Choice overload; poor availability fit | Search-to-menu-view rate | Need-state and budget filters |
| Evaluation | Compare restaurant/dish | Inconsistent menu data, unclear fees/ETA | Menu-to-cart rate | Total-price and arrival confidence |
| Cart | Build order | Item unavailable, substitutions unclear | Cart abandonment | Availability recheck and clear alternatives |
| Checkout | Pay confidently | Fee surprise, payment failure | Checkout completion | Upfront payable total |
| Preparation | Know order is progressing | Status may not reflect actual preparation | Prep-time error | Status updates tied to meaningful events |
| Delivery | Receive correct order | Delay, address confusion, food condition | On-time and fulfilment rate | Better exception handling |
| Recovery | Resolve failure | Unclear eligibility or repeated contact | Resolution time, repeat contact | Guided self-service with human escalation |
| Retention | Repeat with low effort | Past order not suitable now | 30-day repeat rate | Reorder and preference memory with controls |
| Referral | Recommend service | Trust not earned | Referral conversion | Ask only after positive experience |

## Emotional states
Possible emotions such as anticipation, uncertainty, frustration, or relief are hypotheses and should be validated through interviews and in-context feedback.

## Research plan
Observe 8–12 participants across the four hypothesis segments. Ask them to complete a realistic task; record task success, time, misclicks, price comprehension, ETA interpretation, and confidence. Do not use this small qualitative sample to estimate population-level rates.
