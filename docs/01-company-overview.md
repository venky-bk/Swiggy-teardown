# 01 — Company overview

## What Swiggy does
Swiggy is an Indian on-demand convenience platform with a food-delivery marketplace and adjacent businesses such as Instamart, Dineout, and membership/partner propositions. This teardown focuses on food delivery rather than treating the entire company as one product. See the official [Investor Relations hub](https://www.swiggy.com/corporate/investor-relations/).

## Product-level value proposition
- **Consumers:** discover meals, compare restaurants, place an order, pay, and receive food at a chosen location.
- **Restaurants:** digital discovery, order demand, and potential incremental sales.
- **Delivery partners:** access to delivery work through platform-mediated order allocation.
- **Advertisers/brands:** access to relevant discovery placements, subject to platform rules.

## Business model (high-level)
Potential revenue streams for a food marketplace include restaurant commissions, advertising/promoted placements, consumer fees, delivery-related fees, membership economics, and other disclosed arrangements. The exact mix and economics vary by period and are not inferred here without a specific filing. See the annual-report source hub in [S1](../research/sources.md).

## Strategic context
Publicly documented initiatives include Bolt (rapid delivery for suitable items), 99 Store (value), Eco Saver (lower-cost delivery mode), EatRight (health-focused discovery), and MCP/voice-led commerce. Sources: [S3–S8](../research/sources.md).

## Dependencies
- Restaurant availability, menu accuracy, preparation time, packaging, and order acceptance.
- Delivery-partner supply, routing, weather/traffic conditions, and safe operating practices.
- Payments, mapping/location, communications, cloud infrastructure, and support operations.
- Consumer trust in price, ETA, food quality, and issue resolution.

## Product strategy versus company strategy
Company strategy spans multiple convenience categories and shared customers. The food product strategy should optimise meal discovery, fulfilled order quality, repeat usage, and sustainable order economics—not maximise cross-category engagement at any cost.
