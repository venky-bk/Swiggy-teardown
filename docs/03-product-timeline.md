# 03 — Public product timeline

| Date | Publicly documented change | Strategic significance | Evidence / uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oct 2024 | Bolt launched for selected quick-prep food, with a 2 km radius at launch | Narrowing menu and geography can reduce operational variability | Official launch announcement; see S2. |
| Dec 2024 | Bolt announced expansion to 400+ cities/towns | Indicates rapid expansion of the proposition | Company-reported rollout; exact city-by-city availability may change. |
| May 2025 | Bolt announced at 500+ cities; Swiggy reported >1 in 10 food orders via Bolt | Speed proposition becomes a scaled acquisition/retention lever | Company-reported metric; causality and current level unknown. |
| Jul 2025 | 99 Store launched with selected ₹99 dishes and Eco Saver delivery | Affordability can be a distinct discovery proposition | Official announcement; availability/eligibility can vary. |
| Aug 2025 | AGM transcript referenced Bolt, SNACC, Eco Saver, AI allocation/routing, MaxSaver and self-service ads | Signals focus on speed, value, efficiency, and partner monetisation | Leadership commentary; does not disclose detailed architecture. |
| Jan 2026 | EatRight launched as an umbrella for health-oriented food choices | Need-state-based discovery beyond cuisine/restaurant browsing | Official announcement; menu attributes depend on partner data. |
| Jan 2026 | MCP integration announced across Food, Dineout and Instamart | Conversational interfaces can become an additional transaction surface | Official announcement; adoption and conversion impact unknown. |
| Apr 2026 | Multilingual voice-led commerce partnership with Sarvam announced | Voice and local languages may lower interaction barriers | Official announcement; language coverage and live performance require verification. |

Sources and exact URLs: [`research/sources.md`](../research/sources.md). This timeline records public announcements, not every product change or internal decision.
