# 05 — Jobs to Be Done

## Primary job statement
“When I need a meal soon, I want to compare suitable options by total cost, likely arrival, and fit for my needs, so I can place an order confidently without wasting time or being surprised.”

## Functional jobs
- Search or browse restaurants and dishes.
- Filter for price, cuisine, dietary attributes, ratings, and delivery timing.
- Confirm availability and final price.
- Track fulfilment and coordinate receipt.
- Get a fair resolution for missing, incorrect, damaged, or late orders.

## Emotional jobs
- Feel in control of cost and timing.
- Feel confident that the chosen restaurant is reliable.
- Avoid the frustration of repeatedly checking an uncertain order.

## Social jobs
- Choose a meal acceptable to family, colleagues, or friends.
- Be dependable when coordinating a group meal.

## Triggers
Hunger, time pressure, work/study break, social gathering, craving, weather, travel, or inability to cook.

## Switching and abandonment hypotheses
- Switch if another option provides a better total price, ETA confidence, restaurant selection, or support recovery.
- Abandon if fees appear late, menus are unavailable, delivery time is unsuitable, payment fails, or trust in the ETA drops.

## Priority outcomes
1. Minimise time from intent to confident choice.
2. Improve successful fulfilment and order accuracy.
3. Reduce price/ETA surprises.
4. Increase repeat orders without relying only on discounts.

These outcomes should be tested with interviews, usability sessions, event data, and controlled experiments.
