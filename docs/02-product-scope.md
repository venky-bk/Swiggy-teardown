# 02 — Product definition and scope

## Product
Swiggy Food is a digital marketplace and fulfilment coordination product connecting consumers, restaurants, payment systems, and delivery partners.

## Core user problem
People want a meal that fits their need, budget, time, taste, and dietary preferences without spending too much effort choosing, coordinating, paying, and resolving failures.

## Core loop
Need/occasion → discovery → comparison → cart → transparent checkout → restaurant preparation → delivery coordination → meal received → feedback/recovery → repeat order.

## Primary jobs
1. Find a relevant meal with low decision effort.
2. Know the likely total price and arrival window before ordering.
3. Receive the correct meal in acceptable condition.
4. Recover quickly when something goes wrong.
5. Reorder a trusted meal with minimal effort.

## Included
Consumer discovery, restaurant/menu choice, cart and checkout, ETA and order status, fulfilment coordination, post-order support, retention, marketplace analytics, AI-enabled discovery.

## Excluded
Detailed Instamart operations, dine-in booking operations, internal employee tools, proprietary ranking models, private infrastructure, and claims about internal Swiggy APIs.

## Core product principles
- Accuracy over artificial certainty.
- Total-price transparency before commitment.
- Speed should not depend on unsafe delivery behaviour.
- Recommendations should be explainable enough to build trust.
- Restaurant and delivery-partner outcomes are product guardrails.
