# 11 — Business model

## Marketplace participants
Consumers, restaurants, delivery partners, payment providers, advertisers, and platform operations all contribute to the transaction.

## Value exchange
- Consumers exchange payment and attention for choice, convenience, and delivery.
- Restaurants may pay platform commissions and/or advertising fees in exchange for demand and operational tools, subject to their contracts.
- Delivery partners provide fulfilment capacity and receive platform-mediated earnings under applicable arrangements.
- The platform coordinates discovery, order routing, payment, status communication, and support.

## Revenue and cost categories to examine
**Potential revenue:** restaurant commissions, ads/promoted placement, consumer/delivery fees, membership-related economics, and other services disclosed in filings.

**Potential variable costs:** delivery-partner payouts/incentives, payment processing, refunds/credits, customer support, discounts funded by the platform, and order-level operational costs.

**Potential fixed/semi-fixed costs:** engineering, cloud and data infrastructure, product/design, city operations, trust/safety, and corporate overhead.

Exact Swiggy segment revenue, allocation methods, and order-level costs must be sourced from the relevant filings; this document does not invent them.

## Business-model tensions
- Promotions can increase order frequency but lower contribution.
- Ads can monetise discovery but degrade trust if relevance and labelling are weak.
- Faster delivery can improve perceived convenience but may increase operational pressure.
- Membership can improve retention but has a cost that must be balanced against incremental behaviour.
- Better issue recovery can raise short-term support cost while protecting long-term retention.

## Business model canvas summary
- Customer segments: consumers, restaurant partners, advertisers.
- Value proposition: discovery and coordinated fulfilment.
- Channels: app, web where available, conversational/voice interfaces where enabled.
- Key activities: marketplace liquidity, fulfilment coordination, ranking, payments, support, trust/safety.
- Key resources: partner network, brand, technology, operational capability, customer relationships.
- Key partners: restaurants, delivery partners, payment/map/cloud providers.
- Cost structure and revenue mix: validate against current annual report and quarterly filings.
