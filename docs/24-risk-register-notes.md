# Risk register notes

The machine-readable register is [`../data/risk-register.csv`](../data/risk-register.csv). Likelihood and impact ratings are initial qualitative estimates for the proposed Meal Confidence concept, not a formal Swiggy enterprise risk assessment.

Scale:
- Likelihood: Low / Medium / High.
- Impact: Medium / High / Critical.
- Any Critical impact risk should have an explicit owner, mitigation, and launch gate.
