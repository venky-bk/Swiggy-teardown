# 08 — Competitive landscape

## Competitor set
- **Direct:** Zomato (Eternal's food-delivery marketplace).
- **Substitutes:** restaurant direct ordering, restaurant-owned apps/websites, phone ordering, pickup/takeaway, home cooking, workplace/college cafeterias.
- **Adjacent competitive pressure:** QSR chains with owned delivery channels; quick-commerce apps for ready-to-eat and snack occasions.

## Comparison matrix (high-level, public-facing; verify by city/app version)
| Dimension | Swiggy Food | Zomato Food | Direct restaurant ordering | Home/cafeteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Restaurant discovery breadth | Marketplace model | Marketplace model | Limited to one brand/restaurant | Limited/local |
| Cross-restaurant comparison | Core marketplace job | Core marketplace job | Usually limited | Not applicable |
| Delivery coordination | Platform-mediated where offered | Platform-mediated where offered | Varies by restaurant | User-controlled |
| Promotions/membership | Swiggy offers and membership propositions vary | Zomato offers/membership propositions vary | Brand-specific | Usually none |
| Menu/ETA transparency | Must be checked in current experience | Must be checked in current experience | Varies | Often direct |
| Control over preparation | Limited | Limited | Potentially more direct communication | High |
| Convenience | High when supply and delivery are reliable | High when supply and delivery are reliable | Can be high for repeat brand orders | Depends on context |

## Strategic interpretation
The category is a two-sided marketplace where restaurant density and delivery operations can matter as much as app design. Feature parity is likely in core search, checkout, and tracking; differentiation must be measured through local availability, total value, reliability, partner supply, recovery quality, and repeat behaviour.

## What to verify in a live benchmark
Use the same address, time, restaurant, basket, and payment method across platforms. Record displayed ETA, item prices, all fees/taxes, discounts, membership effects, support path, and actual delivery outcome. Do not compare screenshots captured under different conditions.
