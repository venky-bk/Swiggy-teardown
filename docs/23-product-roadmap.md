# 23 — Product roadmap (planning estimates)

| Phase | Indicative duration | Deliverables | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Research and baseline | 2 weeks | Interviews, matched-task benchmark, funnel baseline, data audit | Problem confirmed; baseline and guardrails agreed |
| 2. Prototype | 2 weeks | Clickable prototype and copy variants | Users correctly understand total price and ETA uncertainty |
| 3. MVP build | 4–6 weeks | Price breakdown, ETA display, provenance, recovery entry point | QA, accessibility, privacy, and fallback tests pass |
| 4. Controlled pilot | 2–4 weeks | Limited eligible cohort and experiment dashboard | No guardrail breach; sufficient sample reached |
| 5. Evaluation | 1–2 weeks | Experiment readout and qualitative follow-up | Causal interpretation and segment impact reviewed |
| 6. Scale / revise / stop | Depends on results | Staged rollout or redesign | Primary metric and economics meet pre-set criteria |

Durations are estimates, not commitments. Dependencies include pricing/checkout APIs, ETA reliability, menu-data quality, analytics instrumentation, support operations, and legal/privacy review.
