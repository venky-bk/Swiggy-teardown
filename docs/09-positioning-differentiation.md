# 09 — Positioning and differentiation

## Working positioning statement
For consumers who need meals delivered with low effort, Swiggy Food is a restaurant-discovery and fulfilment marketplace that coordinates selection, payment, and delivery across participating restaurants.

## Potential differentiators to validate
- Local restaurant density and availability.
- Reliable ETA and exception handling.
- Affordability propositions such as 99 Store and Eco Saver.
- Fast-food occasions served by Bolt.
- Cross-category membership and shared consumer relationship.
- Emerging conversational and multilingual ordering surfaces.

## Defensibility analysis
- **Network effects:** potentially local and operational; more consumers can attract restaurants, and more restaurants can improve selection. Effects are not automatic if supply quality or delivery capacity is weak.
- **Data advantage:** potentially useful for ETA prediction, personalisation, and demand forecasting, but data volume alone is not a moat; quality, feedback loops, and governance matter.
- **Switching costs:** likely modest for many consumers; habitual reorder, membership benefits, and saved preferences may increase convenience but should not be treated as lock-in without evidence.
- **Brand/distribution:** brand awareness and app habit may support acquisition; quantify with awareness and organic acquisition data.
- **Operational know-how:** routing, batching, restaurant preparation coordination, and exception handling may be hard to replicate at scale, but internal implementation is not publicly known.

## Positioning map proposal
A defensible map could use **total value (price + reliability)** on one axis and **choice breadth** on the other. Populate it only after standardised mystery-shopping across matched locations and occasions. Do not assign invented coordinates.
