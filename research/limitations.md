# Research limitations

- No live Swiggy app walkthrough or screenshots were supplied; the UX audit is a checklist and hypothesis register, not a claim of observed defects.
- No customer interviews or primary research were conducted.
- Internal metrics such as search-to-order conversion, ETA error, cancellation rates, customer lifetime value, and contribution margin are unavailable here.
- Public announcements describe selected launches and company-reported outcomes; they do not reveal the full product architecture or causal impact.
- Competitive feature availability varies by city, time, user account, and app version.
- Market-size estimates differ by definitions and publishers; this project avoids using a single market estimate as a proxy for Swiggy's addressable opportunity.
- The proposed solution and RICE estimates are hypotheses to validate, not a claim that Swiggy has this problem or should build this feature.
