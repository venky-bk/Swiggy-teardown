## Important interpretation notes
- S3's retention comparison is reported by Swiggy; without the experimental design and confounder controls, it should not be interpreted as causal evidence.
- MCP and voice commerce announcements establish a public product direction, not the exact model architecture, user adoption, conversion lift, or operational reliability.
- Product launch dates and availability can differ from present-day availability. Verify in the app before publishing claims about current rollout.
